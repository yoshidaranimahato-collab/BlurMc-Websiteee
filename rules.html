Server Rules & Guidelines

💔 Lifesteal SMP Rules

  • Combat logging is strictly prohibited (Result: Perm-Kill).
  • Unfair modifications (KillAura, Fly, X-Ray) lead to permanent ban.
  • Heart trading / Alting for free hearts is not allowed.

🌲 Survival SMP Rules

  • Griefing claimed builds is strictly banned.

⚔️ Practice Rules

  • Auto-clicking (above 15 CPS) or macro usage is banned.
  • Refusing a SS (Screen Share) will result in a 30-day ban.
  • Toxic behavior and abuse in duels are penalized.
